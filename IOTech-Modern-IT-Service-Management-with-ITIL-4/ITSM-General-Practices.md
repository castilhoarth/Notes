# ITSM - General Practices

## Fundamental Concepts

* Services: enables value co-creation by facilitating customers outcomes and freeing them from risks and costs management
* IT Service: Services mainly based on Information Technology
* Utility: Functionality offered by a product
* Warranty: How is the service attending and meeting its requirements
* Customer: Person who defines the requirements. Not necessarily a user
* User: The one who uses the service
* Sponsor: Whoever authorizes budget for a service consumption
* Risk: event that could cause harm or loss
* Value: Perceived benefits
* Cost: Amount of money spent
* Output: tangible or intangible deliverable of an activity
* Outcome: It's the result for the stakeholder enabled by outputs

## What is project

It's a temporary endeavor
It has a clear start and objectives that lead to an end

### Services vs Projects

* Software Dev vs Maintenance
* Services outshine projects
* Projects for new services will be within the scope of ITSM
